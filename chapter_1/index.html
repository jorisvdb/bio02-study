About this chapter

A short chapter (10 pages) that builds directly on meiosis from BIO01 chapter 7. Read page 1.1 first: it reminds you of the words (homologs, sister chromatids, meiosis I and II, n and 2n) that the other two pages use. The example exam opens with two questions from this chapter (Klinefelter, Down syndrome). The chapter quiz is not available yet.
