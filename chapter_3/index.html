Chapter 3 – Human pathogens: viruses

Plain-language guides, one per topic. Each section has a Dutch summary (Samenvatting), and each page ends with what the exam expects, a list of questions and answers, a To memorize box and a mini-quiz with hidden answers. Pages work offline.

About this chapter

A short chapter (11 pages) about viruses only: what they are made of and how they replicate inside a host cell. The syllabus refers to a recording "HIV reproductive cycle". Two example-exam questions (8 and 9) are about reverse transcriptase and retroviruses, so page 3.3 is the one to know best. The chapter quiz is not available yet.
