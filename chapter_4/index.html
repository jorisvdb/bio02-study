Chapter 4 – Tools and techniques in molecular biology

Plain-language guides, one per topic. Each section has a Dutch summary (Samenvatting), and each page ends with what the exam expects, a list of questions and answers, a To memorize box and a mini-quiz with hidden answers. Pages work offline.

About this chapter

The longest chapter (70 pages): a catalogue of laboratory techniques. For every technique the pages give the same five things: the principle, what you need, the steps in order, what it is used for, and one trap. Six of the fifteen example-exam questions come from this chapter (PCR, cDNA, cloning tools, transgenic organisms, microarrays), so the exam likes "which technique for which question" and "which component for which technique". Sections 4.10.1 and 4.10.2 of the syllabus are empty headings; the pages say so and give only the basic idea. Model organisms (4.1) were covered in BIO01 chapter 1 and are only summarised.

Learning objectives of the chapter

  1. Describe the principle, methodology and applications of nucleic-acid-based technologies: PCR, FISH, microarrays, sequencing technologies, metagenomics and transcriptomics. → 4.3, 4.9 to 4.13
  2. Describe techniques for the analysis of proteins and metabolites: mass spectrometry, chromatography, proteomics and metabolomics. → 4.7, 4.8, 4.13
  3. Use these concepts to solve problems about biological processes at the molecular level. → the "which technique for which question" tables on 4.12 and 4.13, and the mini-quizzes
