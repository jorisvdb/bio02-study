Techniques: PCR, cloning a gene, and reading DNA

Three animations of chapter 4. Press ▶ Play to watch, or go step by step with ◀ and ▶|. Click 🔇 Turn voice on to hear every step. Keys: Space play/pause, ← → step, Home restart, V voice. Simplified and not to scale: a primer is about 20 bases, a plasmid a few thousand base pairs, a read 75 to 900 bases.

Exam hooks: for PCR you add nucleotides, primers and polymerase; restriction enzymes cut, DNA ligase joins; reverse transcriptase makes cDNA from mRNA; a ddNTP stops a chain for ever, an Illumina terminator is reversible.
