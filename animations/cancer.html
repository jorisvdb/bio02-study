Cancer: how one cell becomes a tumour, and why one hit or two

Two animations of chapter 2. Press ▶ Play to watch, or go step by step with ◀ and ▶|. Click 🔇 Turn voice on to hear every step. Keys: Space play/pause, ← → step, Home restart, V voice. Simplified and not to scale: a real tumour has a billion cells before it can be felt, and takes 10 to 20 years to get there.

Counting rule: a proto-oncogene needs one hit (dominant: the gas pedal sticks), a tumor suppressor gene needs two hits (recessive: the brake only fails when both copies are gone).
